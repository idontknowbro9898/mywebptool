    Your images stay on your device

    The converter reads the file from your device, redraws it on an in-memory canvas and saves it in the format you chose. No image data is sent to our servers. You can check this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab and convert a file. You will see no upload request. You can also load this page, switch off your internet connection and convert files offline.

    Good to know before you convert

    • Metadata is removed. Camera details and GPS location (EXIF) are not copied into the new file. This protects your privacy, but copyright fields are dropped too.
    • Animated images: only the first frame is converted.
    • Colour profiles: embedded profiles are not kept, so images that use an unusual profile may look slightly different.
    • WebP size limit: the WebP format supports up to 16,383 pixels per side.
    • Browser support: Chrome, Edge and Firefox can create WebP files. Some Safari versions can open WebP but cannot create it, and the tool tells you when that happens.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this image converter really free?

    Yes. There is no signup, no watermark and no daily limit set by this site.

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. The conversion runs inside your browser using the canvas feature built into it. Your files are read from your device and the result is created on your device.

    Which formats can I convert?

    You can open PNG, JPG and WebP files and save them as WebP, JPG or PNG.

    Is there a file size limit?

    This site sets no limit. The real limit is the memory of your device, so very large photos of tens of megapixels can fail on older phones.
