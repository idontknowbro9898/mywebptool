Frequently Asked Questions

Will quality drop?

At 82-85% WebP looks identical to JPG 90% but 30% smaller. WebP's VP8 codec is more efficient. Try side-by-side, you won't see difference until below 70%.

What about EXIF?

We strip EXIF by default (GPS, camera model). This protects privacy and further reduces size 5-50KB. If you need copyright, keep original.

Best quality setting?

Photos: 80-85% sweet spot. Portraits: 82%. Landscapes: 85%. E-commerce: 85-90% to avoid artifacts on white backgrounds.

Can I batch?

Yes, 50 JPGs at once. Each converts locally, you get total saved % display.
