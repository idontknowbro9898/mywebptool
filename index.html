Frequently asked questions

Is the image converter free?

Yes. These browser tools require no account or payment and add no watermark. Each batch is limited to 20 images and 100 MB of input for browser memory management.

Can I convert images offline?

Once the page and its conversion script have finished loading, conversion can run without a network connection while that tab remains open. Reopening the site offline is not supported.

Does converting to PNG improve image quality?

No. PNG avoids another lossy encoding step, but it cannot restore detail already lost in a JPEG or lossy WebP image.

What if the converted image is larger?

The tool reports the increase. Keep the original, lower WebP or JPG quality, or reduce dimensions if appropriate. PNG quality cannot be changed with the quality slider.
