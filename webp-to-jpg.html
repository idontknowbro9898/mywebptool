Use JPG when your destination requires it

Some upload forms, print workflows and older applications require JPEG. Converting WebP to JPG provides a compatible copy, but it introduces lossy JPEG encoding and removes transparency.

How to convert WebP to JPG

  1. Select static WebP images.
  2. Choose JPEG quality. Leave width blank to keep dimensions.
  3. Convert, inspect the white background and image detail, and download the JPG results.

Transparent backgrounds become white

JPEG cannot store an alpha channel. This converter fills the canvas with white before drawing your image. For logos or cutouts that need transparency, use WebP to PNG instead.

Expect a size and quality trade-off

A JPG copy may be larger or smaller than the source WebP. Lower quality can reduce file size at the cost of artifacts. Check text, flat colors and sharp boundaries carefully.

Frequently asked questions

Can JPG keep transparency?

No. JPG has no alpha channel. This tool uses a white background for transparent and partially transparent pixels.

Is JPG output lossless?

No. JPEG encoding is lossy, including at the highest quality setting. Keep the source WebP or choose PNG to avoid a further lossy encoding step.

Can I convert animated WebP to JPG?

The output is a single still frame. This tool cannot retain animation or export every frame.
