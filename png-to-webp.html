Frequently Asked Questions

Will transparency be kept?

Yes. WebP fully supports alpha. Your PNG transparent background stays transparent. We use canvas with premultiplied alpha preserved.

How much smaller?

Typical: Screenshot PNG 1.2MB → WebP lossless ~400KB (66% saved), WebP lossy 85% ~180KB (85% saved). Photo PNG 3MB → WebP 85% ~280KB.

Lossless or lossy?

For graphics/text choose lossless (quality 100). For photos/screenshots with gradients choose 85% lossy - looks identical but much smaller.

Is WebP supported everywhere?

Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari 14+, Android, iOS 14+. Only very old Safari/IE lacks. For fallback, keep PNG original.
