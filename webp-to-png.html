    When PNG is the right choice

    • Your editing program, design tool or app does not open WebP files.
    • You need transparency in a format that practically every program understands.
    • You want to edit the image repeatedly without losing more detail on each save.

    Why the PNG is larger

    Most WebP images use lossy compression. They discard detail that is hard to see, so they are small. PNG keeps every pixel, so a photo saved as PNG can be several times larger. Converting does not bring back detail that the WebP no longer contains. It only stops any further loss.

    Things to know

    • Animated WebP: only the first frame is converted.
    • Metadata: camera details and embedded colour profiles are not copied to the PNG.
    • Need a smaller file? Use the WebP to JPG converter for photos that do not need transparency.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why is the PNG so much bigger than my WebP?

    PNG stores every pixel without loss, while most WebP files are lossy and much more compact. The PNG holds the same picture in a heavier format.

    Does converting to PNG restore the quality of a lossy WebP?

    No. Any detail already removed from the WebP stays gone. PNG only makes sure no further quality is lost.

    Is transparency kept?

    Yes. Transparent areas of the WebP stay transparent in the PNG.

    What happens to animated WebP files?

    Only the first frame is converted. This tool creates a single still PNG.
