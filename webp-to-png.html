Frequently Asked Questions

Is PNG bigger?

Yes. PNG is lossless, so file grows 2-5x vs WebP. That's expected. You're trading size for editability and compatibility.

Will transparency stay?

Yes. WebP alpha → PNG alpha 100% preserved.

When to use?

Editing in Photoshop, uploading to sites that don't accept WebP, printing, need lossless archive.
