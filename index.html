Good to know

  • EXIF removed: GPS and camera data stripped for privacy. Copyright fields also removed — add back if needed.
  • Animated images: Only first frame converted (canvas limitation).
  • Color profiles: Embedded ICC not preserved — slight shift possible on wide-gamut images.
  • WebP limit: 16,383px per side max per spec.

FAQ

Is this converter really free with no limits?

Yes. No signup, no watermark, no daily cap. We limit to 50 files per batch only to avoid browser memory crash on phones. Cost is zero because we use your CPU, not our server.

How do I prove my images aren't uploaded?

1) Open DevTools (F12) → Network tab. 2) Convert an image. 3) You'll see zero upload requests. 4) Turn off WiFi after page load, conversion still works. Code is open in script.js — it uses toBlob() locally.

Which quality should I use?

For photos: WebP 80-85% looks identical to JPG 90% but ~30% smaller. For logos with text: WebP lossless or PNG. For screenshots: PNG or WebP lossless. Try 85% then lower until you see artifacts.

Will transparency be kept?

PNG → WebP: Yes, alpha preserved. WebP → PNG: Yes. Anything → JPG: No, becomes white background because JPG has no alpha channel. We fill with white.

Why does Google like WebP for SEO?

WebP reduces LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) and bandwidth. Google's PageSpeed Insights flags large JPG/PNG. Converting to WebP at 85% often moves LCP from Poor to Good, which is a ranking signal since 2021 and even stronger in 2026.
