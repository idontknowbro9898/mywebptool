Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

Images selected for conversion

The supplied converter code processes images in your browser. It does not upload image bytes or filenames to an image processing service. It creates temporary browser URLs for previews and downloads and releases them when files are cleared or replaced. Downloads remain on your device until you delete them.

Source metadata

The converter redraws decoded image pixels and does not deliberately copy source EXIF, GPS, copyright fields or embedded profiles. Browser encoders may add their own metadata. Do not treat conversion as certified anonymization: visible details in a photo and its filename may still identify someone.

Cookies and local storage

The supplied site code does not set cookies or store images or preferences in localStorage. It does not include advertising or analytics scripts.

Hosting and network requests

Opening a website sends requests to its hosting provider. Those requests expose information such as your IP address, requested URL and browser headers. Hosting or security providers may keep logs, set security cookies or inject analytics independently of this site's files.

The public site was observed using Cloudflare services during the November 2026 review. Any enabled Cloudflare Web Analytics or security features operate separately from image conversion. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Local image processing does not mean that loading the website generates no network requests.

Support messages

If you contact us by email, your email provider and the receiving mailbox process your address and message. Do not include private images. You may ask about a support message using contact@myimgconvert.com.

Your choices

You can clear the selected files, close the tab, remove downloaded files, and use browser privacy controls. After the page and script load, conversion can continue with networking disabled in that open tab. Reopening the site offline is not supported.
