Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-15

Core promise: Your images never leave your device. All conversion happens locally via Canvas API. No upload, no server storage, no sharing.

What we collect: Nothing about your images

  • No image data uploaded
  • No file names stored
  • No EXIF kept - we strip GPS, camera info by default

What we may collect (anonymous)

  • Basic analytics: page views, country (via Cloudflare), device type - no IP stored, no personal data
  • No cookies for tracking. Only necessary localStorage for quality preference.

Third parties

We may show minimal ads to cover domain cost. Ads never see your image data. Ad networks see page URL only.

Your rights

Since we store nothing, there's nothing to delete. You control everything locally.
