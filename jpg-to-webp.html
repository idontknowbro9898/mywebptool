    How much smaller is WebP?

    Google's WebP documentation reports that lossy WebP images are 25% to 34% smaller than JPEG images at equivalent quality. In practice the saving depends on the photo. Detailed images with lots of texture shrink less than smooth ones. This tool shows the exact numbers for your files before you download anything.

    Things to know

    • Convert from the original if you can. A JPG that was already compressed loses a little more detail when it is compressed again.
    • Metadata is removed. This includes GPS location, which is useful when you publish photos online.
    • Faster pages. Photos are often the largest element on a page, so smaller files help it load sooner. See the format guide for how to add WebP safely.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I lose quality when converting JPG to WebP?

    A little. Both formats are lossy, so the image is compressed a second time. At quality 80 to 85 the difference is usually hard to see. If you still have the original file, convert from the original.

    Will the EXIF data and GPS location be kept?

    No. The converted file does not contain camera details or GPS coordinates. That helps privacy, but copyright fields are removed as well.

    Can I upload WebP images to WordPress?

    Yes. WordPress has supported WebP uploads since version 5.8. Check any plugin or theme that processes images if you see problems.

    How much smaller will my photo get?

    It depends on the image. Google reports that lossy WebP is 25% to 34% smaller than JPEG at comparable quality. The tool shows your exact saving before you download.
