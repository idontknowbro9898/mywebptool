What WebP to PNG conversion changes

PNG encodes the pixels decoded by your browser without an additional lossy compression step. That does not make the source WebP lossless or recover missing detail. Browser color handling and optional resizing can affect the pixels.

How to convert WebP to PNG

  1. Select WebP files from your device.
  2. Leave maximum width blank to preserve dimensions, or enter a smaller width.
  3. Convert and download PNG files individually or together.

Why there is no PNG quality setting

Browser PNG encoding ignores the quality slider. To make a smaller PNG here you can reduce dimensions, which also removes detail. To keep dimensions with lossy compression, consider WebP or JPG output in the compressor.

Animation becomes a still image

This converter draws a single frame to a canvas. It does not preserve WebP animation or export APNG. Retain the animated source for workflows that require motion.

Frequently asked questions

Does PNG improve a blurry WebP?

No. PNG preserves the browser-decoded pixels without another lossy encoding step. It cannot undo earlier compression or blur.

Why is my PNG larger than the WebP?

PNG and WebP use different encoders. A lossy WebP photo can be much smaller than a PNG storing its decoded pixels.

Does the PNG retain a transparent background?

Yes, the output PNG retains the decoded alpha channel unless the input itself has no transparency.
