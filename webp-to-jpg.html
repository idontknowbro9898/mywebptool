Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to transparency?

JPG has no alpha, so transparent pixels become white. We fill canvas white before export.

What quality?

For conversion from WebP, use 90% to avoid double compression artifacts. If source was lossy WebP, don't go below 85%.

Why convert back?

Some platforms: eBay, Amazon listings, some PDF tools, old CMS still require JPG. Also email attachments.
