Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I save?

JPG photo at 85% → 80% saves 20-40% more. PNG screenshot → WebP 80% saves 70-85%. Unoptimized phone photo 6MB → 400KB at 80% WebP.

Does it reduce quality?

You choose. 80-85% is visually lossless for web. Below 60% you start seeing artifacts. PNG → WebP lossless keeps perfect quality but still saves 30-60%.

Best for SEO?

Compress to WebP 80-85% for photos, WebP lossless or PNG for graphics with text. Aim <200KB per image, <100KB for above-the-fold.
