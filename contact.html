Useful details for a bug report

  • The page or tool you used.
  • Your browser version and device or operating system.
  • The source format, approximate file size and image dimensions.
  • The output format, quality, resize settings and exact error message.

Do not email sensitive photographs, identity documents or client images. A non-sensitive sample that reproduces the issue is preferable if you choose to provide one.

Before reporting a problem

Check the supported formats and limits. Try a smaller batch on a device with available memory, and keep your original images.
